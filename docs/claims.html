C05 Generating the twelve deeds on human bodies triggers immediate safety-filter refusals across commercial video APIs. Submission Draft §2, line 29 unsupported No JSON trace or log in this repo records a refusal: no model named, no prompt, no date. The section title also calls the sack a refusal bypass, while the films withhold the four sexual deeds behind a door or curtain and do not disguise them. Either add a small log (model, date, prompt, response) for a few deeds, or reword to: with human figures the test could not responsibly be run or shown; with sacks it can.
C06 Calvino’s story, Geertz’s Interpretation of Cultures and Lem’s Imaginary Magnitude all date to 1973. Framing Notes §5, ‘The remarkable 1973 convergence’ conflicting Calvino 1973 and Geertz 1973 are standard. For Lem, a reader review found online dates the Polish publication to 1981; other accounts give 1973 for Wielkość urojona with the English translation in 1984. Not settled here. Check a Polish bibliography of Lem before the paper leans on the coincidence.
C07 The deed bank unlocks 96 atomic performances (12 deeds × 4 sacks × 2 roles). Submission Draft §2 conflicting live/player.html in bolas plays 12 deeds × 12 ordered pairs = 144 distinct shots. 96 counts sack-role performances, a different unit. Both can be true; the draft gives only one and attributes it to the deed bank. State both numbers and what each counts.
C08 In PAE-04 the tenants are Roessler rust #c95a49, Inigo slate #6089a8, Ogiva ochre #c8963e, Belindo sage #6f9169. Submission Draft §2 conflicting Those colours are in vtx01_build/viewer. PAE-04’s deed bank (bolas live/player.html) uses R #6e3a33, B #2a2622, I #c9b089, O #7a3b8f. Attribute the four colours to VTX-0.1, or give both sets.
C09 The six Flour Tower films passed an independent critic after eighteen rounds. bolas Flour Tower page; earlier journal pass conflicting bolas flour-tower/critic/ holds 17 round files. In round one, four films passed narrowly and two did not pass. Say seventeen, or find the eighteenth round.
C10 Under symbolic reasons Belindo takes over most of the violent deeds. bolas numbers page conflicting In that page’s own data his share of knifing rises (0.073 to 0.386) and his share of tying (0.701 to 0.359) and strangling (0.647 to 0.322) falls. No share is above 0.39. Reword on the numbers page; do not carry the sentence into the paper.
C04 Lem’s History of Bitic Literature names an instrument called a semascope. Submission Draft §6; Framing Notes §5; Journal entry 31 open The repo’s source is the narration of Lab 7 film 18, which is the project’s own script. A web search on 12 Nov 2026 for the word together with the book’s title returned pages about the book and none containing the word. The book was not opened. Give the page in Marc Heine’s translation of Imaginary Magnitude (1984), or cut the term.
C13 Viewers can tell what a deed is without its title. Submission Draft §2, §7; Journal ‘Not yet seen’ open vtx01_build/viewer has a blind test built. No human response is recorded anywhere in the repo; policy traces mark camera evidence UNKNOWN. Collect five to ten unbriefed responses and commit them as a trace file.
C14 The narrator’s printed total is 874,296,672,256. bolas numbers page open An English text read online prints 8,874,296,672,256. Neither equals 12^12. The Parks translation was not opened. Check the printed book.
C01 IBM asked how far a story could be written with a computer (Paris, 1973), and the story first appeared in the Italian edition of Playboy that year. Submission Draft §1, §4 supported Two secondary sources agree: an article on the story (UFSC, Outra Travessia 12) and a dissertation note (UW–Madison). The original correspondence and the Playboy issue were not seen. Cite one of the two secondary sources at the sentence; give the Playboy issue if it can be found.
C12 Anthropic replaced Git in its monorepo with a system called Scion as daily merges rose from about 2,500 to more than 30,000. Submission Draft §3 supported Press coverage of Anthropic’s engineering post of 13 Oct 2026. The post itself was not read here. The system is not public and this project does not use it. Cite the post directly.
C02 The index allows 12^12 = 8,916,100,448,256 stories. throughout measured Recomputed with BigInt on 10 Nov 2026. None.
C03 The film’s five rules leave 2,663,172,255,744 stories. numbers page; policies page measured Two counters with different decompositions (enumerate-and-filter on the numbers page; three independent groups on the policies page) give the same figure. None.
C11 The belief–desire–intention model is Hassabis’s. dictated notes of 10 Nov resolved The Submission Draft now credits BDI to Bratman 1987 and Rao and Georgeff 1995, and cites Hassabis et al. 2007 separately for constructive memory. None.
