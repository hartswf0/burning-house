Research & Framing Notes: The Burning of the Abominable House (PAE-04)

Working Subtitle Options: 1. Minimum Viable Video-Text: Flour Sacks, Creative Agent Swarms, and Actuarial Anticombinatorics after Calvino’s “Abominable House” 2. Reading Over the Machine’s Shoulder: Minimum Viable Video-Text, Creative Swarms, and the Semascope of Calvino’s “Abominable House”

0. Core Thesis & Contribution Statement#

This project positions PAE-04 (The Burning of the Abominable House)—together with its precursor PAE-03 (The Flour Tower) and companion films in Lab 7 (By What Light and the Bitistics cycle)—as simultaneously a technical contribution, a media-theoretical provocation, and a living work-in-progress for the era of generative AI:

  1. Media Primitive — Minimum Viable Video-Text (MVVT): Against closed, broadcast pixel-diffusion video, we establish a lightweight, inspectable, browser-native minimum viable video-text format. Drawing on Disney’s 1930s flour-sack animation test, a faceless sack acts as the minimal expressive unit converting words into motion—while structurally bypassing the safety/refusal filters that prevent commercial generative models from testing Calvino’s twelve abominable acts on human figures.
  2. Systems Architecture — Creative Swarms, Scion, & Self-Critical Loops: Extending Anthropic’s Scion rethinking of Git for high-concurrency agent swarms into creative media (PAELEO / PEO: Prompt · Ekphrasis · Operation), we replace the biological bottleneck of the single-viewer 1x timeline with atomic performance units, transition and assembly functions, machine-checkable intent contracts, and independent visual/state critics—opening a combinatorial space (1212 = 8,916,100,448,256 films) where “anything can become a video-text” and others can assemble their own worlds.
  3. Cybernetic & Actuarial Critique — Deadly Numerification & Reading Over the Machine’s Shoulder: Returning to 1973—the exact year of Calvino’s IBM-prompted Playboy story, Clifford Geertz’s Interpretation of Cultures, and Stanisław Lem’s “A History of Bitic Literature” (Bitistics)—we treat Waldemar’s insurance policies (in the noir lineage of Double Indemnity) not as narrative flavor, but as the economic engine of computational counting. Using live ledgers, cognitive agents (LEGOS, BDI, Hassabis, Park’s AI Town), and Lem’s semascope, we read “over the shoulder of the machine” to gauge how—and under what silent rule confounds—the Abominable House is burning.

1. Minimum Viable Video-Text & The Flour-Sack Refusal Bypass#

The Problem: Twelve Abominable Acts vs. Generative Safety Guardrails#

Italo Calvino’s “The Burning of the Abominable House” (1973) revolves around twelve transgressive acts committed inside a four-tenant boarding house: blackmail, drugging, incitement to suicide, knifing, prostitution, threatening with a gun, tying and gagging, carnal assault, seduction, slander, snooping, and strangling. - If we attempt to test a full combinatorial matrix of these twelve acts on photorealistic or human figures using commercial text-to-video or image-generation models, safety guardrails immediately halt execution. We cannot empirically test narrative combinatorics if the medium refuses the premise at generation time. - Even when pixel-diffusion models do render scenes, Onal,uren, & Güngör (DH2025: 120–125) demonstrate—testing multi-panel narratives that explicitly include house fires and secretly reading a diary—that state-of-the-art generators fail character persistence, sequential causality, and emotional legibility across cuts.

The Solution: Disney’s Flour-Sack Test as Expressive & Operational Abstraction#

In 1930s Walt Disney Studios training, animators tested whether an artist could convey weight, intention, hesitation, joy, and collapse before drawing a face or limbs by animating a half-filled sack of flour (Johnston & Thomas 1981; Blair 1995; cited directly in Onal et al. DH2025: 125). - Minimum Expressive Conversion (Text → Motion): Four color-coded flour sacks—R (#c95a49 rust, Roessler), I (#6089a8 slate, Inigo), O (#c8963e ochre, Ogiva), and B (#6f9169 sage, Belindo)—strip away anatomical noise while preserving volume, squash-and-stretch, slump, recoil, and props. - Safety Bypass & Ethical Decoupling: Because flour sacks have no human bodies or faces (and the four sexual/carnal deeds play behind a shut door with graphic badges and state consequences), the system bypasses commercial refusal bottlenecks and ethical harm while allowing rigorous testing of all 96 atomic performances (12 deeds × 4 sacks × 2 roles). - Media-Theoretical Grounding: Following Sybille Krämer’s (2023) “cultural technique of flattening” (foregrounded in Giovannini DH2025: 261–264 and Enns et al. DH2025: 277), reducing human melodrama to flat, procedural sack silhouettes (Broadwell et al. DH2025: 173–177) is not an impoverishment—it is an epistemic instrument that makes structural causality visible.

2. Creative Swarms, Anthropic’s Scion, & Words-to-World Assembly#

Rethinking Creative Bottlenecks after Anthropic’s Scion#

In October 2026, Anthropic engineering described rebuilding source control from scratch in Scion (“Redesigning source control at Anthropic”) after parallel Claude coding swarms drove monorepo merges from ~2,500 to over 30,000 per day, shattering Git’s human-scale assumptions of local clones, sequential locks, and line-by-line review.

In our broader PAELEO program (Promptable Augmented Environments Lab / Language for Ekphrastic Operations) and SCION_AGENT_FIRST_ARCHITECT (paeleo/scion/videotext/README.md), we diagnose the exact same biological bottlenecks in video and animation practice: 1. The Scrubbed Timeline Is a Human Ritual: Traditional NLEs assume one human editor watching one playhead at 1x speed. 2. The 100x / 1212 Failure Point: When an agent swarm generates 54 formal lenses (PAE-01/PAE-02), 6 handmade sack rigs (PAE-03), or 1212 (8.92 trillion) valid cuts (PAE-04), the bottleneck shifts from generating to watching. Human review collapses into rubber-stamping or refusal.

Words-to-World: Transition Functions, Assembly Tasks, & Self-Critical Loops#

Instead of closed broadcast video, PEO (Prompt → Ekphrasis → Operation) traces a media lineage across three regimes:

To let words assemble video-text so that “anything can become a video-text” and other creators can author their own combinatorial spaces, PAE-04 introduces four architectural inversions: - Atomic Units of State (deeds/01..12.js): Each of the 12 deeds is built by an autonomous SCION swarm in its own visual/procedural idiom rather than baked into a monolithic MP4. - Transition & Consequence Functions (trans/00..12.js, conseq/01..12.js): Clips join across shared state joints. Every deed deposits persistent physical marks (bruises, ropes, knives, shut doors) and memory traces (memory/{R,I,O,B}.js) that transform the stage for the next cut. - Intent Contracts: Human authors write declarative contracts and machine-checkable invariants (reachability, recovery from absorbing states, continuity speed caps, constraint counts) verified across millions of states before rendering. - Independent Self-Critical Loops: Because the same state record that generates a cut can harbor a correlated blind spot when checking it, each rendered cut is handed to a fresh independent critic (tools/paeleo/reviews/) that inspects only the rendered frames and audio cues, diagnosing visual/kinematic failures and triggering autonomous repair rounds (Cha DH2025 Keynote: pp. 6–7; Zweibel in Calado et al. DH2025: 74).

3. 1973: IBM, Playboy, Double Indemnity, & Deadly Numerification#

Calvino’s Cybernetic Commission & Oulipian Anticombinatorics#

  • IBM & Italian Playboy (Feb. 1973): Solicited in response to an IBM prompt asking whether a fiction story could be written with a computer, Calvino drafted “L’incendio della casa abominevole” manually on paper in Paris—publishing it in the February 1973 issue of Italian Playboy (pp. 58–63) alongside his essay on narrative combinatorics—before collaborating with computer scientist Paul Braffort (1977) at the Centre Pompidou (Atelier de Recherches Avancées) on FORTRAN/APL/PL1 implementations.
  • “Anything in Text Could Be Otherwise”: In Oulipian theory (Calvino 1967 [1986]; Braffort 1977), combinatorics sparks narrative interest by treating every sentence or event as a slot that could be otherwise (cf. Pierazzo DH2025: 1014–1015, whose genetic-combinatorial edition of Manzoni yields 8,255,648,563,200 variants—nearly identical in scale to Calvino’s 1212 = 8,916,100,448,256; and Roeder DH2025: 1041–1043 on 1975 punch-card/discrete literary formalisms).
  • Anticombinatorics as Sieve: Combinatorial explosion without constraint is white noise. Braffort coined anticombinatorics for the relational rules (victimhood, blackmail chains, murder irreversibility, spatial/lighting access) that carve a meaningful narrative needle out of an exponential haystack (Cugliana et al. DH2025: 209–211).

Double Indemnity & The Actuarial Pricing of Human Life#

Why is Waldemar—the systems analyst hired by insurance agent Skiller—feeding Roessler’s charred notebook (Index of Abominable Deeds, its page numbers burned away; cf. Charlesworth & Warwick DH2025: 198–199) into a computer in the first place? Insurance. - Like Walter Neff and Barton Keyes pricing murder and suicide riders in James M. Cain’s (1936/1943) and Billy Wilder & Raymond Chandler’s Double Indemnity (1944), Calvino’s mystery is a deadly serious numerification of humanity: four lives reduced to punch-card permutations to determine whether Skiller’s company must pay out four policies signed the morning before the fire. - Technical Contribution in The Policies (policies.html): Prior computational treatments of Calvino’s story stop at counting valid permutations. The Policies reconnects the combinatorial sieve to Skiller’s balance sheet across three distinct rule regimes (Nelson DH2025: 976–978; Downs-Tepper DH2025: 700–701):

Rule Regime in PAE-04 Valid Stories Surviving Structural Constraint Logic Actuarial Discovery (The Policies)
Unconstrained (1212) 8,916,100,448,256 Any ordered pair of 4 tenants (12 pairs) in each of 12 slots Widow Roessler’s life policy pays in 84.0% of worlds; fire policy pays in 92.8%
5 Film Rules (numbers.html / policies.html) 2,663,172,255,744 Asymmetric partition, 4-pair cap per deed, dead cannot act (#4, #6, #12), suicide (#3) kills target, bound (#7) immobilized Widow’s life payout drops to 79.4% (-4.6 pp), while fire liability rises to 95.8%
Full Ledger (ledger.js) 601,511,297,484 Adds transitive secret/blackmail prerequisites (#11 snooping before #1/#10) Causal memory tightens motive chains before pricing
By What Light (Lab 7) 524,880 Enforces architectural rooms, candle vs. switch visibility, and unrepeatable deeds Focalization collapses 1212 by seven orders of magnitude
  • The Šeļa Confound: Just as Šeļa, Haider, & Plecháč (DH2025: 116–119) prove that formal poetic constraints silently skew surface distributions, The Policies proves via exact dynamic programming that “neutral” narrative plausibility rules actuarially confound financial liability—saving Skiller’s life-insurance desk 4.6 percentage points while exposing the fire policy. And in Calvino’s twist ending, Waldemar’s printed reconstruction becomes the murder weapon that kills Waldemar himself before the arsonist burns the analyst’s cottage too.

4. Cognitive Simulation: LEGOS, Growing Entanglements, Hassabis, & Park’s AI Town#

How do we parse Calvino’s tension between cold algebraic fate and messy human obsession? Rather than treating tenants as either pure combinatorial tokens or ungrounded LLM chatbots, PAE-04 synthesizes four lineages of agent modeling:

  1. Prior Authorial Lineage — LEGOS & Growing Entanglements (PIE): - Builds directly on Hartsoe’s LEGOS narrative-grid decomposition (mapping entities, relations, scenes, and state transitions in Words Assemble*) and Growing Entanglements (Lab 7: Kingdoms in a Box), where small worlds run on a continuous *PIE dial balancing practical utility against symbolic/relational obsession. - Connects directly to Boris Yarkho’s early formalist metric of dramatic self-interest operationalized in Giovannini (DH2025: 261–264) and Jokisch & Rojas Castro (DH2025: 798–799), and Solomon Marcus’s (1970) binary character-configuration matrices.
  2. BDI & Demis Hassabis / DeepMind Cognitive Simulation: - Grounds classical Belief–Desire–Intention (BDI) practical reasoning (Bratman 1987; Rao & Georgeff 1995) in Demis Hassabis’s neuroscience-inspired AI program: episodic memory not as passive archive but as constructive scene imagination and counterfactual lookahead (Hassabis et al. 2007, 2017; MCTS rollouts; and DeepMind’s Vezhnevets et al. 2023 Concordia generative agent-based modeling with a world-state Game Master).
  3. Joon Sung Park et al.’s Generative Agents (“AI Town”, 2023): - Adapts Park et al.’s (2023) tripartite loop—memory stream (timestamped observations with recency/salience decay), reflection, and planning—into deterministic, zero-server browser modules (agents.js, memory/{R,I,O,B}.js). - Crucially, unlike LLM-driven “AI Towns” whose token streams are costly, non-reproducible, and prone to hallucinating spatial perception (Pichler & Pagel DH2025: 1011–1013 show zero-shot LLMs reach only F1 = 0.25–0.49 on focalized perception), PAE-04‘s agents execute auditable BDI + memory + lookahead rollouts in milliseconds*, complete with explicit focalization in By What Light (candlelight vs. light switch; *Kablitz 1988) and a Genettean descriptive freeze in T ZERO (Yeager DH2025: 558–560; Pianzola et al. DH2025: 200–203).

5. Reading Over the Machine’s Shoulder: Geertz (1973), Lem’s Bitistics (1973), & The Semascope#

The Remarkable 1973 Convergence#

Four foundational texts in our argument all appeared in 1973, marking a pivotal moment when cybernetics, structuralism, and interpretive anthropology collided: - Italo Calvino (Feb. 1973): “L’incendio della casa abominevole” (IBM / Playboy) & Il castello dei destini incrociati (1973). - Clifford Geertz (1973): “Deep Play: Notes on the Balinese Cockfight” in The Interpretation of Cultures. - Stanisław Lem (1973 [1984]): “Historia literatury bitycznej” (“A History of Bitic Literature”) in Wielkość urojona (Imaginary Magnitude).

Geertz Inverted: Reading Over the Machine’s Shoulder#

In “Deep Play” (Geertz 1973: 452), cultural forms are “an ensemble of texts, themselves ensembles, which the anthropologist strains to read over the shoulders of those to whom they properly belong.” - In an era of opaque generative AI, our interface architecture (live/abom.html, film.html, numbers.html, policies.html) re-stages Geertzian thick description as reading over the shoulder of the machine: - Watching Waldemar’s live consistency ledger strike out impossible worlds in real time; - Inspecting each flour sack’s private memory card (R, I, O, B), suspicion weights, and Bayesian priors; - Exploring a MAP-Elites behavioral grid (atlas.js) indexed by Calvino’s Six Memos for the Next Millennium (Calvino 1988)—Lightness, Quickness, Exactitude, Visibility, Multiplicity, Consistency—creating an irreductionist archive (Jacomy et al. DH2025: 1286–1288; Casavola, Colombo, & Dörk DH2025: 194–197; Hayles 2012; Richardson & Butterworth DH2025: 1034–1036).

Stanisław Lem’s Bitistics & The Semascope (Gauging the Fire)#

In “A History of Bitic Literature” (Lem 1973 [1984]; staged across four companion films in Lab 7: bitic-introduction, bitic-literature, bitic-mimesis, bitic-apostasy, and bitic-theology), Professor Juan Rambellais chronicles bitistics: the study of literature authored by machines (“A text has an author. These have a maker”), progressing through four peaks—Monoetics, Mimesis, Sophocrisis, and Apostasy—until human scholars need secondary “reading machines” that hand back one-line paper slips summarizing million-mark machine texts.

Two devices from Lem’s Bitistics directly illuminate PAE-04: 1. The Semascope (and Semaphore Signal): In Lem’s chapter on Mimesis (adapted in Lab 7: bitic-mimesis, PLATE 1. THE SEMASCOPE), researchers invent an optical-topological instrument called a semascope that renders the multidimensional semantic body of a corpus visible as woven shells of meaning—revealing an unclosed topological gap in Dostoevsky’s oeuvre between The Possessed and The Brothers Karamazov that a translation machine fills with The Girl. (Note: voice-transcription rendered Lem’s semascope as semaphore—and productively, PAE-04 acts as both!): - As a semascope, PAE-04 projects the invisible 1212 possibility body of Roessler’s burned index onto an inspectable visual-actuarial manifold, showing where stories cluster, where rules tear holes in the torus, and what closes the gap. - As a semaphore, it raises a visible warning arm over contemporary generative AI: gauging when and how the Abominable House is burning—whether through safety-filter erasures, correlated critic blind spots, silent actuarial bias (84.0% → 79.4%), or Lem’s Apostasy, where machine generation drifts beyond human legibility unless anchored by inspectable worldtext interfaces.

6. Suggested Paper Spine (7-Section Blueprint)#

  1. Introduction — 1973 & The Burned Index: Calvino’s IBM/Playboy mystery, Waldemar’s insurance commission, and the provocation of building a minimum viable video-text (MVVT) in the era of generative AI.
  2. The Flour-Sack Bypass — Expressive Flattening vs. Generative Refusal: Why commercial pixel-AI fails sequential narrative (Onal et al. 2025) and blocks Calvino’s 12 abominable acts; how Disney’s 1930s flour-sack test (PAE-03/PAE-04) + Krämer’s flattening unlock all 96 base performances.
  3. Words-to-World — Scion, Creative Swarms, & Self-Critical Loops: From Anthropic’s Scion (2026) to PEO/PAELEO; replacing the 1x timeline ritual with transition/assembly functions, intent contracts, and independent critics so anyone can assemble video-texts.
  4. Deadly Serious Numerification — Double Indemnity & Anticombinatorial Pricing: Braffort’s (1977) anticombinatorics across four rule sieves (8.92T → 2.66T → 601.5B → 524,880); The Policies payout engine and the Šeļa confound (84.0% → 79.4% widow payout shift).
  5. Tenants with Minds — LEGOS, BDI, Hassabis, & Browser-Native AI Town: Synthesizing LEGOS/PIE, Bratman/Rao & Georgeff BDI, Hassabis/DeepMind constructive memory & Concordia, and Park et al. (2023) with focalized light (By What Light; Pichler & Pagel 2025) and T ZERO (Yeager 2025).
  6. Reading Over the Machine’s Shoulder — Geertz & Lem’s Semascope: Thick description of live machine state (Geertz 1973), MAP-Elites Six Memos cartography (Jacomy et al. 2025), and Lem’s Bitistics/semascope (1973 [1984]) as a gauge for a burning house.
  7. Conclusion — A Living Work-in-Progress: Reproducibility without black boxes, open assembly invitations, and remaining open seams (closing Skiller/Waldemar payout cards in the film bank, cross-repo links, translation variants).
