SCION 41 · critic round 2 · PASS (narrow)

Method. I rendered /home/claude/pae03/films/41-soft-body.html headless at 1280×720. I called PAE_READY first, then took PAE_SEEK frames every 0.5 s across 0–32 s and every frame in the ten named windows (381 frames). I read contact sheets, 2× crops of frames 27, 168, 264, 372, 437, 518, 528, 553, 648 and 744, and crop strips of the approach (f240–324) and the cut-3 brace (f430–436). I checked 390×844 once. A second full simulation produced an identical PAE_TRACE, so the film is deterministic. I judged the picture before opening PAE_CHECK and round1.md. I read the source only for the hooks, the cards, and the tower-lean and penny code, and I probed SIM.frames for ring contacts and grain pooling. Files are in /home/claude/pae03/critic/41/r2/ (f/, sheets/, w/, trace.json, check.json).

The ten choices. Flinch (1.0): reads. At 1.04–1.17 the sack squashes, tips back and hops left about 0.17 m, and its ears flick and settle by 1.8. Flinch less (4.0): reads, weak. There is a smaller squash and tip at 4.08–4.30 with about 5 cm of travel. It is visibly less than the first, but small at full frame. Notice (6.5): reads. The tower snaps to about 3.7° of lean over 6.2–6.6, and the first sifting grains fall at 6.6–6.7. At 6.67–6.83 the ears go upright and the body tips toward the tower. The cause now comes before the gesture. Come, head low (9.0): reads. At 9.17–9.4 the sack pitches forward with its top below its trailing shoulder. It then crosses in about six real hops: its lowest ring point rises 8–13 cm off the table at f225–237, f264–273, f303–312 and f327–336, and contacts stay planted between hops. It reaches x 0.09 by 14.0. Prop (14.5): reads, narrowly. Over 14.5–15.0 the sack flattens its right flank into the lower half of the tower’s leaning face, and the measured lean stops at 12.4°. On screen this is still contact stopping a slow creep rather than a fall being held. Brace (18.0): weak. The sack drops about 10% in height at 18.04–18.25 and its centre holds within ±0.8 cm, so it no longer gives ground. But in the same three frames (f432–434) the tower jumps from 12.4° to 2.9° and stands nearly upright, as if the knife stroke straightened it. The cause is the code’s “push beyond holding rights the tower” term (TBK), and it is not on the card. The brace reads as the sack pushing the tower back up in a jerk, not as the sack holding under a blow. Leap (21.4): reads. It crouches at 21.0–21.4, lifts off by 21.5 and stretches to about 0.76 m tall, but the motion floats at 1.8 m/s². Catch (22.4): reads. The penny comes down onto the sack’s top at 22.42–22.50. Land (23.0): reads. It lands in a wide squash at 23.1–23.2 at x 0.81–0.84, and the pile bursts to both sides. The penny has slid to the right top corner at the ear root (x 0.97 against the sack’s centre at 0.81) and stays there to 32 s. Gather (24.5): reads strongly. Grains lift off both piles from 24.6 in two arcing streams that meet over the mouth by 26–27.5. The sack rises to 0.94 by 28.5, the pile goes from 426 to 11 to 0, and the total stays 753. Stay (30.0): reads. The knife descends to hover over the penny at 29.8–30.0, holds, and withdraws at 31.25–31.6. The sack does not move.

Does it read as a flour sack. Mostly. The bottom pools in most frames: the share of grains in the lower half is 0.68–0.72 at rest, 0.69 at the prop and 0.70 at the end. During the approach (f240–336) the silhouette now bends over its middle, with the top folding forward over a heavier base. That is an improvement on round 1’s rigid tip, though the curve stays mild. Two corners are always visible. Failing frames: f27–f34, where the flinch tips it like a can. f516–f536, the leap, where it is an upright capsule with ears standing like horns and a lower-half share of 0.52–0.55, so the base is not visibly heavier. The full sack from 28.5 on is a rigid striped crock with straight sides and horn-like ears, more jar than slack sack. When the ears are raised they are stiff spikes, but they do drag and catch up after the flinches, the hops and the landing.

Lineage. The sack is built and moved in the simulation’s terms. It is a 48-point Verlet ring with stretch, bend and area constraints, filled with 327 colliding grains and two spring-chain corners. Travel now comes from real push-off hops with planted contacts. Volume holds within 1% and flour is conserved with 0 escaped. The cards now state honestly that the penny flick is fixed and blind to the sack (PVX 0.04 in the code), that the leap aims at the penny, that the caught penny is pinned to the cloth, and that a gather flow field moves grains at the mouth. One scripted behaviour is still unnamed: the tower is righted by about 10° in 0.08 s at cut 3. Pose targets do most of the acting, but that is within the lineage’s muscle model.

Craft defects. The grains are beads, so the tower looks like a column of pearls and the piles like popcorn, not flour. The dust is flat grey puffs. The low gravity gives the collapse and the leap a lunar float, with grains hanging over 21.0–22.5. The tower jerks upright at f432–434. The penny finishes at the corner beside the ear, half on the shoulder, rather than on the top. Mobile at 390×844 lays out correctly: film in the top band, controls and cards below, no page scroll. But the picture is about 180 px tall inside a black stage, which leaves the penny and ears tiny.

PAE_CHECK. Every row HOLDS: flinch 1.08 and 4.08, notice 6.67, comes 9.17, props 14.58, braces 18.13 (0.0 cm retreat), leaps 21.54, catches 22.46, lands 23.17 at x 0.83, gathers 24.58, stays 30.0. Volume is 0.999–1.008 before the gather, and flour is 753/753. Timing agrees with the picture. The check does not see the tower righting at cut 3, the penny’s resting point at the corner, or the brace’s small visible compression under spray.

Round 1 conditions. 1 (notice has a visible cause): met. The tower is at 3.7° with sift visible before the ears rise at 6.67. 2 (brace without retreat, at least 10% compression readable): mostly met. There is no retreat and about 10% compression, but the new tower snap undercuts it. 3 (approach travel accounts for itself): met. Hops of 8–13 cm lift, with planted contacts between them. 4 (curved line of action, heavy base in leap): partly met. The approach bends over its middle, but the notice pose is a straight tilt and the leap base is not heavier. 5 (catch earned or declared): met. The flick is fixed, the leap aims at the penny, and the card declares the pin.

Verdict: PASS (narrow). Repair conditions, ranked: 1. Cut 3 must not straighten the tower. Over 18.0–18.6 the tower’s lean must change by no more than 1.5°, or any righting must be named on the card and spread over at least 0.5 s. 2. The penny must end on the top. From 23.2 to 32.0, the penny’s x must stay within 0.10 m of the sack’s centroid x and lie on the top seam, not at the ear root (currently 0.15–0.24 m off-centre). 3. Weight must stay low in the leap and the full stand. The lower-half grain share must be at least 0.62 at 21.6–22.4, and from 28.5 on the outline must show a visible bulge or sag in its lower third rather than straight crock sides. 4. The prop must hold a fall the viewer can see. Before contact at 14.5 the lean rate should be at least 2°/s, and it should drop to under 0.2°/s within 0.5 s of contact. The sack’s contact should reach above the tower’s mid-height. 5. The flour must read as flour. Grain radius should be at most 1.2 cm, or the grains should be drawn as a merged powder surface, and the dust should be fine particles rather than grey discs. Gravity should be at least 4 m/s², or the card should state the slow gravity as a stylistic choice.
