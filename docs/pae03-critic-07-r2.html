SCION 07 · critic round 2 · PASS

Method. I rendered /home/claude/pae03/films/07-foldes.html headless at 1280x720 through window.PAE_SEEK. I saved the film canvas every 0.5 s from 0 to 32 s (/home/claude/pae03/critic/07/r2/frames/) and every frame in the ten windows the brief names (/home/claude/pae03/critic/07/r2/win/, 350 frames). Contact sheets are in /home/claude/pae03/critic/07/r2/sheets/. Enlarged crops of the flinch, notice, approach, prop, brace, leap/catch and gather are saved in /home/claude/pae03/critic/07/r2/ as *_zoom.png. I checked the phone layout at 390x844 (phone.png). I wrote the per-choice judgments below from the picture before I read PAE_CHECK or round 1. I read the source only to find PAE_SEEK and to check the claims on the cards. The only network failure was the blocked Google Fonts request.

Flinch (1.0): reads. The knife lands at 1.00. The sack squashes and is thrown left (x −1.30 to −1.48 by 1.33), its ears trailing, then it recovers by 1.7. Flinch less (4.0): reads. At 4.04–4.33 the squash is visibly shallower and the slide is 0.06 m, compared with 0.18 m for the first flinch. Notice (6.5): reads. At 6.75–7.0 the rear ear rises and the front ear lifts toward the tower. By 8.5 the body has tilted right and holds that pose to 9.0. The cause is now on screen: the tower tilts visibly from about 7.0 and leans 4.6° by 9.0 (0.080 rad), with flour sifting off its face. Come, head low (9.0): reads, and it is still the best passage. From 9.1 the contour alternates between a tall wedge and a long low wedge whose front ear points down toward the tower. The rear and front bottom corners plant in turn, like an inchworm, so the travel is accounted for and nothing slides. Prop (14.5): reads, with weaknesses. From 14.25 the front flank lies flat along the tower’s face. At 14.42–14.58 the front ear runs up the face nearly to the penny. The tower holds at 6.8° from 14.6 to 17.9, while its unheld lean would have reached 10.4°. The pose is tall and narrow, not “spread wide”. Brace (18.0): reads, narrowly. Under the knife, at 18.04–18.25, the front ear and top drop (top −4.5 cm, highest point −6.3 cm) and the tower is shoved back from 6.8° to 4.3°. The sack does not retreat (x 0.393 to 0.394). Beside 17.83 the frame at 18.58 is clearly different, but most of the difference is the tower moving; the sack’s own change in outline is small. Leap (21.4): reads. The sack crouches at 21.33, launches at 21.46–21.54, and from 21.67 to 22.33 stretches tall and lifts about 10 cm off the table, up along the falling tower’s face. Its horizontal travel toward the penny before the catch is small (about 6 cm). Catch (22.4): reads. The penny slides off the tilting top and drops into the dip between the ears at 22.42–22.5. It stays there through the descent and the squash at 23.0. Land (23.0): reads. It is a hard squash at x 0.80 with a ring of flour strokes, and the ears snap a few frames late. Gather (24.5): reads. From 24.6 the two humps shrink while blue-grey strokes and faint ghost outlines of the pile rise into the sack. The sack grows steadily to 0.95 by 28.5, and the pile is gone by about 28.2. Stay (30.0): reads. The knife hangs directly over the penny from about 29.9 to 31.2 and withdraws from 31.25. The sack does not move a point.

Is it a flour sack in every frame? Yes in most frames: the bottom pools, the sides pinch in, and the corners trail. The full sack from 28.5 on reads as a heavy filled bag that is also the tower’s outline, which is the film’s point. The weak frames are these. 13.92–14.08: the front of the sack juts at mid-height into the tower face as a sharp beak. 21.46–21.54: at launch the top is drawn flat and lidded, like a bucket. 7.0–9.0 and 21.67–22.33: the two corners are drawn as sharp points rising off a narrow body, so the silhouette reads as a sitting cat’s head and ears more than as a sack’s tied corners. None of these frames loses the sack entirely. The round-1 mushroom recovery (23.13–23.29) is gone: those frames are now a squat pillow whose top is no wider than its base.

Lineage. The sack is built and moved in the Foldès language, not restyled. I checked the cards against the source. N = 64, and the segment counts put the bottom at 0, the corners at 22 and 42, and the top at 32, as claimed. KEYORDER lists 22 sack keys. The tower/pile set has 7 keys: three cut states, two collapse keys, the pile and the low pile. Each sack frame is lerpP between two keys plus the disclosed vertical rescale. The corner points are weighted toward the pose 3 frames earlier. Travel is integrated from a planted corner, or from a parabolic height while airborne. In the gather, ghost outlines lerp the pile’s contour point for point into the sack’s. PAE_CHECK reports 0 self-crossing frames for the sack and for the tower/pile. Nothing in the picture contradicts that. Two claims are slightly inexact. (a) The note says the slices’ area “equals the flour the sack already holds”. The figures are 0.258 against 0.2691, so the full sack (0.5634) is 2% larger than the uncut tower (0.5523). (b) The leap snaps X to the tower’s x on its last airborne frame, which the “parabola” wording does not mention. Neither is visible on screen.

Craft defects. (1) Tower snap at 13.83–14.0: when the sack’s front first touches the face, the tower jumps from 6.9° to 1.7° in four frames, then re-leans to 6.8° by 14.6. It reads as a wobble, not as a hold, and PAE_CHECK records it only as “contactStopsLeanFrom 13.833”. (2) The fallen pile is still two symmetric humps with a valley exactly where the sack lands (22.75 on). It reads as a construction made to match the sack’s corner indices, not as a fallen heap. (3) The prop is narrow and tall, not spread wide. (4) The catch is passive: the penny slides to the sack more than the sack goes to the penny. (5) At 22.42–22.7 a faint line of the tower’s outline shows through the sack’s fill. (6) After cut 3 a thin red cut line stays in the tower from 18.3 to 21.0, in the knife’s colour, as if the knife left part of itself behind. (7) The dust from the slices settles flat on the table line and is almost invisible after 2 s. It is there, but the story that the flour is conserved is carried by the numbers more than by the picture. Phone layout: the film fills the top half, the controls and cards are below, and the page is exactly 390x844 with no page scroll. The scene band inside the film area is small: the sack is about 50 px tall.

PAE_CHECK against the picture. Every onset matches what I saw to within a frame or two: flinch 1.042, flinch less 4.042, notice 6.667, come 9.125, prop 14.333, brace 18.042, leap 21.458, catch 22.417, land 23.0, gather 24.625, stay 29.875, withdraw 31.25. The rest also agree with the picture: volume ratio 1.000 after the rescale (raw blend 0.963–1.057), flour balance (pile 0.2943 = sack gain), penny max y 0.951 during 21.0–22.4, brace x 0.393 to 0.394, and end x 0.80 at height 0.95 with the penny on top. Where the check overstates the picture: “prop HOLDS” does not register the snap at 13.83, and “brace HOLDS” is mostly carried by the tower being pushed back, not by the sack’s own outline.

Round 1 repair conditions. (1) Tower lean by 9.0: met. The angle is 0.080 rad, the lean is visible in still frames, and the lean is constant from 14.6 to 17.9. (2) Brace: met. The highest point drops 6.3 cm, x at 18.5 is not less than at 17.9, and 18.0 and 18.4 can be told apart at contact-sheet size, though narrowly. (3) Landing recovery with no top-heavy frame from 23.0 to 23.5: met. (4) Penny never above 1.0 from 21.0 to 22.4: met (max 0.951), and it now drops onto the sack. (5) Slabs break up instead of vanishing: met. At 1.25–1.5 and 4.2–4.5 each slab’s outline visibly breaks into its own strokes, which fall and stay.

Verdict: PASS. All ten choices read on screen. The brace and the prop are the thinnest. Every round-1 condition is met. The sack is built from corresponding-point contours and moved by them, with inchworm travel and a consequential metamorphosis into the tower’s outline.

Repair conditions (refinements, ranked). 1. No tower snap at first contact: from 13.7 to 14.6 the tower’s angle may not change by more than 1° in any 4-frame span. Falsified if THETA drops by more than 0.017 rad within 4 frames anywhere in that window. 2. Make the prop spread wide: at 15.0 the sack’s width at half height must be at least 1.2 times its width in the approach key, with its rear corner set out wider. Falsified if the 15.0 frame’s half-height width is not greater than that of the 13.0 frame. 3. Make the fallen pile asymmetric: the heap must lie mostly on the side the tower fell toward, so it does not frame the sack’s landing spot as a matched valley. Falsified if the pile’s two humps differ in area by less than 25% at 23.5. 4. Draw the corners as tied corners, not sharp ears: in the notice and leap poses (7.0–9.0, 21.67–22.33) the ear tips must be blunt, with an included angle above 50°, so the silhouette cannot read as a cat. Falsified if any ear tip in those windows has an included angle under 50° at its tip point. 5. Hide or clip the tower’s outline behind the sack at 22.4–22.7, and draw the cut-3 mark in the tower’s line colour rather than the knife’s red. Falsified if any tower stroke is visible inside the sack’s fill, or if a red line persists in the tower after the knife leaves.
