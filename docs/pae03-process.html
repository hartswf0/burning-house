PAE-03 · How the cuts and openings were made

Everything here was built from the six self-measuring films in ../films/. The scripts are kept as they ran; their paths point at the working folders they were made in (/home/claude/...), so read them as a record rather than a one-command build.

The world camera (world-camera/)#

Every film records the sack and the penny in its own world metres (window.PAE_TRACE). calibration.json maps each film’s metres to its canvas, measured from known points (the tower at x = 0.8 m, the table at y = 0, the penny at y ≈ 0.95 m). With that, one virtual camera can shoot all six films in a single shared world: a cut between films keeps the sack in the same place, pose and scale, and only the material changes. - film.py is the shot list: story time per shot, which film, camera keyframes or a lock on the current film’s sack, speed ramps, crossfades, shakes. It writes the crop for every frame. - cap.js seeks each film (PAE_SEEK) and screenshots exactly that crop at high resolution. Slow motion is real: the films are sampled between their own frames. - compose.py assembles the frames and titles.

The openings (openings/ and ../live/)#

Three title sequences, each playable live in ../live/: 1. Picture book: ink on paper; the sack pours its own flour into the mug, and the game is acted step by step. 2. Flour grains: about 20,000 grains under one lamp. Upside-down letters are poured, packed, flipped, given a penny, cut on four turns, collapsed; the sack’s hop; the flour rises into the title. 3. Cel tower: the same game, but every brick is an animation cel cut around the sack from one of the six films (atlas.jpg, 24 frames per film, captured by celcap.js), each playing its own loop.

The sound (sound/)#

The flour-sack test for sound is the one-source exercise: score a scene with a single material and no melody to lean on, so feeling has to come from rhythm, density, pitch and weight. Here the one material is the grain: gran.py builds every sound from short windowed grains. Each cel landing is a pitched grain (the tower plays a rising A-minor pentatonic as it stacks), the pour is a grain hiss, the knife and collapse are grain bursts, the title chord is one grain per arriving cel. score_gran.py re-voices the film’s score from grains with the same cue sheet as score.py.

The other cuts#

  • compilation/: the 4-minute cut of all six films with title cards and the YouTube description.
  • frankenstein/: the 40-second cut with sewn seams and 59 cuts across bodies under one score.
  • short/: the vertical short, framed by hand per shot.
