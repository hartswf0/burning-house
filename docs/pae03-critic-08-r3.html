SCION 08 · critic round 3 · PASS (narrow)

Method. I rendered /home/claude/pae03/films/08-sutherland.html headless at 1280×720. I took stills every 0.5 s across 0–32 s with light-pen goals and console both on (r3/g) and both off (r3/ng), and every frame in the ten requested windows (r3/w, 359 frames). I built contact sheets (sheet_g_, sheet_ng_, sheet_w_*) and read full-resolution frames at 9.5, 11.0, 15.0 (goals on and off), 21.458, 21.75, 22.917, 23.208, 24.0 and 29.0. I also pulled the solved ring of all 768 frames out of the page (rings.json) and ran my own tests on it: ring self-crossing, ear tip inside the ring polygon, penny inside the ring, and ring area. I checked the layout once at 390×844. I read the source only for PAE_SEEK and to verify the cards. I wrote every judgment below before opening PAE_CHECK or round2.md. The only console error was a failed font fetch, which is harmless.

Flinch (1.04–1.30): reads. On the stroke the body squashes into a flat lozenge, the ears flick up (42° at 1.04) and settle by 1.12, and the sack lands 0.19 m to the left at x −1.51. Flinch less (4.04–4.20): reads. It is the same gesture with less squash (height 0.45 m against 0.41 m) and about 0.08 m of travel. Notice (6.67–8.9): reads. The two corners spring up at 6.71, a frame after the tower starts to lean. The body rises a little toward the tower and holds for about 2 s, then lowers its seam at 8.5–9.0. The thinking is visible. Come, head low (9.08–14.3): reads. There are four inchworm cycles of 1.3 s. The top stays at or below 0.53 m, the seam leads low and forward, and each base step carries the travel. Two defects remain here. In the long frames (10.5–13.6) the silhouette is a rounded rear hump with two pricked corners and a crimped toe, so it reads more like a crouching animal than a sack. Also, at each touchdown the base skates 2–3 cm a frame for two or three frames while it is within 5 mm of the table (12.58–12.67, 13.92–14.0). Prop (14.42–17.9): reads. The front rises flat against the leaning face, and the tower’s lean stops at 9.2° while the free lean shown by the ghost keeps rising to 18.7°. The body is boot-shaped at 15.0, but it plainly presses. Brace (18.04–18.6): reads. The top drops 0.13 m at the cut, the base gives up 4 mm, and the tower stays at 9.2°; it is held, not righted. Leap (21.42–22.2): reads. The sack crouches from 21.0 to 21.38 and springs into the air at 21.42. At its peak height-to-width is 0.86, against 0.51 at rest, so it is stretched relative to its slump but not long. Take-off frames 21.417–21.5 are a boot with a crimped toe and two persistence ghosts under it. Catch (22.21): weak. The tower comes apart cleanly at 21.0 and the penny now falls: 0.23 m from 21.0 to 22.2, never inside the ring. But it falls at a constant 0.20 m/s from the first frame, while the sack moves at about 3 m/s. For 1.2 s it hangs beside the sack like a feather (21.6–22.1). The sack then sweeps in under it from the left at 22.21, and the penny stays on the seam through the landing and every later frame. Land (23.0): reads. The sack is a clean pillow at x 0.74 with the penny on top (22.92–23.5), and the ears and seam ends are outside the outline. Gather (24.54–28.5): reads, and is the best passage. Grains stream from both piles into the lower body, the piles shrink to 7.5% of the flour, and the sack rises from 0.48 to 0.93 m into a pear with its weight low and its seam tilted. Stay (29.875–31.29): reads. The sack is still while the knife drops straight down, hovers 0.16 m over the penny and withdraws.

Does it read as a flour sack in every frame? Nearly. My tests found no frame where the ring crosses itself, no frame with an ear tip inside the ring, and ring area within 0.11% of constant outside the gather. No frame is a knot of lines. The weak frames are the take-off boot (21.417–21.5) and the approach frames whose crimped toe and pricked corners read as a crouching animal (10.5–11.4, 12.0–13.6). From 22.9 to 24.5 the sack sits up to 0.23 m into the landing pile; the sack’s fill hides this, so the picture reads as a sack bedded in flour.

Lineage. The sack is built and moved in Sketchpad’s language. It is a 29-point ring held by edge, strut, fold-limit and AREA constraints, with two corner points on springs, floor contact with stick-or-step friction, and a relaxation solver whose iteration count and residuals print on the console. It is driven only by dashed light-pen goals to crosshairs, with a dashed ghost of the tower’s free lean. With goals and console off, every choice still reads. The cards check out in the source: N 29, 6×8 = 48 blocks, SUB 6, TOL 2e-4, MAXIT 24, tower weight 0.22, terminal speed 0.20 m/s, and the gather’s seam goal computed from fill. The flour ledger drifts by 2e-5. One claim is loose. The card says the corner-spring angle is keyed “up to 24.5 s”, but the ear track has keys at 28.5 and 32, and the base goal holds a key to 32.

Score positions. At 14.0 the sack’s centroid is at x −0.21, not +0.40; its leading edge is near +0.40 because this half-empty sack is about 1 m wide. The penny falls from x 0.64, not 0.75, because the tower is leaning. The sack lands at 0.74 and ends at 0.84. None of this breaks invariant 1, because every onset is within ±0.25 s (catch 22.21 against 22.4 is the widest). Invariant 4 also holds: both flinches go left, away from the knife, and the approach starts at 9.08, 2.4 s after the lean begins.

Craft. The right pile still ends in a vertical face at the table’s right edge (23.0–24.5); the pile’s slope test does not look at that edge. The landing flour cloud is visible: about 180 grains through 24.0. Each approach step skates a few centimetres at touchdown. Layout: on desktop the film is left and the panel right; at 390×844 the film fills the top 422 px with controls and cards below, and there is no horizontal scroll. Both pass.

PAE_CHECK, compared after judging. Every row says HOLDS: 1.04, 4.04, 6.67, 9.08, 14.42, 18.04, 21.42, 22.21, 23.00, 24.54, 29.875, 31.29. Volume is 0.999–1.000, the gather is matched (sack +0.3489, pile −0.3489), and the end is x 0.84, top 0.94, penny on top. These agree with my measurements, including 0 ear frames inside and 0 self-crossings. The table is generous only on “catches”: a penny falling at 0.20 m/s passes a check that does not ask whether the fall looks like gravity.

Round 2’s conditions. (1) The penny must fall: met on its terms. Support goes at 21.0, the penny falls 0.23 m before contact, and the catch lands at 22.21. One frame moves it 1.4 mm upward (22.167→22.208), which is negligible. What the condition did not ask for is that the fall look like gravity, and it does not. (2) Ears and seam on the outside: met. My point-in-polygon test finds 0 of 768 frames, and PAE_CHECK now uses the same test. (3) Brace holds without righting the tower: met (9.2° from 14.5 to 21.0, against a pre-cut 9.2°). (4) Landing pile: mostly met. The ridge stays at least 0.016 m below the sack top, the steepest interior slope is 0.45, and the cloud lasts 1.6 s. The vertical face at the table’s right edge remains. (5) Gather completeness and card accuracy: met (7.5% left at 28.5; the card says the seam goal is computed from fill).

Verdict: PASS (narrow). All five round-2 conditions are met or nearly met. Nine choices read and the catch is weak. The sack is never a knot, and it is built and moved in the lineage’s language. The pass is narrow for two reasons. Act 3’s penny drifts instead of dropping. The approach silhouette reads as an animal for about 3 s.

Repair conditions, ranked: 1. The penny drops. From 21.0 until contact its downward speed rises every frame under gravity (no fixed terminal speed under 1 m/s). It falls at least 0.25 m, the catch lands at 22.4 ± 0.25, and no frame moves the penny upward before contact. 2. The approach reads as a sack. In 9.0–14.3 no frame shows a toe crimp: the turn between neighbouring ring edges at the front-bottom is under 60°, and wrinkle ticks are not drawn there. The corner tips sit within 0.05 m of the seam line, so they do not stand up as ears. 3. No skate at touchdown. In 9.0–14.5, whenever any base point is within 5 mm of the table, that point moves less than 4 mm per frame. 4. The take-off is a sack. At 21.40–21.55 the outline has no concave toe, and persistence ghosts are not drawn while the ring moves more than 0.05 m a frame. At peak the height-to-width ratio is at least 1.1, so the leap reads as stretched long. 5. Small truths. The right pile has no vertical face at the table edge in 23.0–24.6. The Moved by card names the actual end times of the ear-angle and base-goal keys.
