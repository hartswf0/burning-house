Forensic & Modal Scoring · story.js

Preposterous, Possible, Plausible, Probable, Certain: How Deeds Are Rated

Every deed in a film carries two distinct numbers in the sidebar—for instance, believability 0.64 PLAUSIBLE · p 0.192 of 12 pairs. Separating them is essential:

  1. Absolute Event Believability (raw ∈ [0, 1]): Measures whether this specific actor doing this deed to this victim right now makes dramatic, physical, and psychological sense—even if all twelve options are terrible (for example, when three tenants are already dead).
  2. Normalized Pair Share (p ∈ [0, 1], summing to 1 across 12 pairs): Computes rawi / Σj=1..12 rawj (where uniform chance is 1/12 ≈ 0.083), and sums log P = Σk=0..11 ln pk along the 12-step tree.
raw(k, A → V) = clamp₀..₁ [ Motive(d, A) · (Target(d, V) / 0.6) × VitalityGate(0.02 if A dead; 0.03 if V dead; 0.05 if A tied) × Overpower(0.3 + σ(1.4(str_A − str_V) + 1.6·weak_V)) [for tying, strangling, assault] × CalvinoLaws(wrestler rules, gun-before-rope, seduction-after-assault, 3-deadly cap) × MindMomentum(1 + 0.9·grudge + 0.3·fear − 0.6·guilt) × BarthesMyth(1 ± 0.6 · Predator_A) ] Film Believability = GeometricMean(raw₀ … raw₁₁) = exp( (1/12) · Σₖ ln max(10⁻⁴, rawₖ) )

Why a geometric mean for the whole film? In a forensic reconstruction, an arithmetic average would let eleven 0.85 deeds hide one impossible 0.02 corpse-acting contradiction (mean = 0.78). The geometric mean penalizes broken links multiplicatively, pulling the film down into POSSIBLE or PREPOSTEROUS the moment causality snaps.

03
Cultural Rationality · Hartsoe (Fall 2024), Growing Entanglements

PIE: Holding Practical Action, Symbolic Myth, and Entropy in View

The three sliders labeled PIE in the Film Bank come directly from Watson Hartsoe's Fall 2024 research monograph, Growing Entanglements: Captain Cook, cultural rationality, and seven small worlds (Probabilistic Cultural Systems Ontology).

When Captain Cook arrived at Kealakekua Bay during the Makahiki festival (17 January 1779) and was killed upon returning with a damaged mast (14 February 1779), anthropology split into two causal worlds: Marshall Sahlins read action inside cosmological and ritual categories (Lono, sacred timing), while Gananath Obeyesekere read Hawaiian actors through practical political calculation (a stolen cutter, hostage leverage, rank, and force). Rather than declaring one ontology winner, PIE gives the disagreement three explicit handles:

  • P — Symbolic Obligation (0) ↔ Practical Pressure (1): At P = 1 (Obeyesekere's pole), tenants choose deeds for leverage, grudge, fear, debt, and muscle advantage (P). At P = 0 (Sahlins / Barthes's pole), tenants act out the mythic masks the house has cast them into (Sy: MISER extorting SCAPEGOAT, IDOL seducing DREAMER, PREDATOR striking VICTIM).
  • I — Collective Schema (0) ↔ Individual Belief (1): At I = 0, a sack judges everyone's strength and danger by the boarding house's shared rumor mean; at I = 1, it acts strictly on what it personally witnessed or uncovered in lit rooms.
  • E — Order / Reorganization (0) ↔ Chance / Breakdown (1): Sets the softmax temperature τ = 0.08 + 1.6E. Low E locks tenants onto their highest-utility compulsion; high E lets breakdown and chance scramble the house.
U(s → v) = P(s,v)^pie.P × Sy(s,v)^(1 − pie.P) × C_intent(s,v) P_choice(pair i | State_k) = exp( ln U_i / τ ) / Σ_j exp( ln U_j / τ ), where τ = 0.08 + 1.6 · pie.E

Bayesian Theory of Mind · Baker, Saxe & Tenenbaum (2009); Chandra et al. (2024)

THE AUDIENCE: Inverse Planning, Author Beam Search, and Crossed Strands

In human cognition (Baker, Saxe, & Tenenbaum 2009), an observer watching silent figures move through rooms performs inverse planning: instead of seeing hidden intentions and predicting deeds, the spectator sees deeds and runs Bayes' rule backward to infer what each agent is secretly plotting. Chandra, Li, Tenenbaum, & Ragan-Kelley (2024, “Storytelling as Inverse Inverse Planning”) go one level higher: a storyteller chooses scenes by simulating what that Bayesian audience will think next (I²P).

10 Hypotheses per sack s: h₀ = "no plan" (prior 0.20) + 9 directed plans {make X pay, win X, make it right with X} for X ≠ s (prior 0.80 / 9 ≈ 0.089 each) • Surprise = −log₂ P_watch(observed pair) / log₂(12) [p = 0.28 ⇒ surprise = 0.51] • Twist = Σ_s KL( P_after(h_s) || P_before(h_s) ) [bits of belief revision across all 4 sacks] • Suspense = H_binary( P_watch(next lethal deed kills) ) [peaks at 1.00 when survival is 50/50] • Irony = P_watch(A plans harm to V) × (1 − Belief_V(harm_A)) [audience knows; victim doesn't!]
Why P(it kills) = 0.00 early?
Only III Incitement, IV Knifing, and XII Strangling can kill—and in ledger.js, Incitement only kills if a victim's accumulated fear+grudge+debt exceeds 1.5, while Knifing only kills a non-Belindo sack already carrying rope/emptied/torn/drowsy! Before anyone is weakened, P(III kills) = 0.00, so binary entropy suspense = 0.00.
Why 0.09 vs (no plan 0.20)?
Before Deed I gives clues, each of the 9 specific plans starts at its exact uniform prior 0.80 / 9 = 0.0889 ≈ 0.09 while no plan holds 0.20. After observing deeds, Bayes shifts weight onto the suspected culprit—and prints · read correctly whenever the audience's top guess matches the sack's true hidden BDI plan!
strands holding: X of 4
From Calvino's Castle of Crossed Destinies: every deed sits in two personal rows of THE TABLEAU (doer & victim). Each sack's own row gets a geometric-mean believability score; it "holds" if believ > 0.30 with zero dead/tied contradictions. weakest strand is the Exactitude axis of Six Memos.

Probabilistic Language of Thought · Goodman, Tenenbaum & Gerstenberg (2015)

PLOT · Tug-of-War Update: Inferring Unseen Strength & Danger

In Noah Goodman, Joshua Tenenbaum, and Tobias Gerstenberg's Probabilistic Language of Thought (PLoT), the canonical textbook program is a tug-of-war tournament: nobody ever directly sees "strength." Observers only see who overpowers whom in each match, and condition a generative model backward to update latent Gaussian distributions over strength and danger.

In ledger.js, every deed is treated as an asymmetric match between Doer A and Victim V:

  • Starting Priors (PRIOR_STR): Belindo starts strongest (μ = +1.5 ⇒ σ(μ) = 0.82), Widow Roessler even (μ = 0.0 ⇒ 0.50), Ogiva slight (μ = −0.4 ⇒ 0.40), and listless Inigo weakest (μ = −0.8 ⇒ 0.31).
  • Match Outcome (y): Normally the doer prevails (y = 1), so unexpected upsets (Inigo overpowering Belindo!) cause large upward jumps in μA and downward drops in μV. The one built-in refusal is trying to strangle the wrestler (XII on B), which fails (y = 0), lowering the attacker's inferred strength!
  • Danger Update (Kalman Filter): Each observer also tracks harm ∈ [0, 1] for A, pulling it toward the intrinsic violence of the verb (knifing = 1.0, assault = 0.9, gun = 0.7, blackmail = 0.3, seduction = 0.0).
  • Epistemic Gate (Knowles & Tenney's House of Dust): Crucially, a tenant O only updates its belief table belief[O] if O knows about the deed—meaning O was A or V, O was present in the same room with light ≥ 1, the deed was public (X Slander), O snooped (XI), or O later walked into that lit room and found its material trace! The sidebar's PLOT · tug-of-war update shows the average across the house, while THE MINDS (B) shows each sack's private belief.
Expected win probability: p = σ(μ_A − μ_V) = 1 / (1 + exp(−(μ_A − μ_V))) Strength update (if known): μ_A ← μ_A + 0.9·v_A·(y − p), μ_V ← μ_V − 0.9·v_V·(y − p) Danger Kalman update: harm_A ← harm_A + (v_h / (v_h + 0.3)) · (HARM[deed] − harm_A)

Semiotics & Expressive Compositing · Barthes (1957, 1970) + ledger.js

BARTHES · The Match & THE FOUR: How Mythic Archetypes Drive the 7 Stage Levers

Why is the archetype readout titled BARTHES · the match? Not only is Belindo Kid a professional wrestler, but in the opening essay of Mythologies (1957), “The World of Wrestling” (Le monde où l'on catche), Roland Barthes famously distinguishes wrestling from boxing: boxing (like Tenenbaum's PLoT!) measures hidden quantitative strength, whereas wrestling is a theatrical spectacle of immediately legible moral archetypes—the Miser, the Bastard, the Suffering Victim, the Idol—where every gesture externalizes a second-order social myth (and in S/Z, recurring semes cluster into a character mask).

In ledger.js, each sack carries a 15-archetype probability vector (st.myth[id]) initialized to its boarding-house persona: Roessler (miser 0.50 · landlady 0.50), Belindo (gentle giant 0.60 · brute 0.40), Inigo (fallen lord 0.60 · dreamer 0.40), Ogiva (idol 0.70 · seducer 0.30). Every deed mixes (α = 0.35, or 0.80 upon death) new roles (PREDATOR, VICTIM, SCAPEGOAT, SPY, INVULNERABLE, CORPSE, GHOST).

What “THE FOUR · archetype and levers” actually does to the video:
Those seven bars (loom, weight, tempo, halo, shadow, shrink, grey) are not passive labels! When the LEVERS button is active in film.html, leverComposite() separates the background, Doer layer, and Victim layer onto offscreen 1920×1080 canvases and uses those 7 numbers to physically scale, light, cast shadows, re-time, and desaturate each flour sack—while passing st.body marks (rope, gag, torn, coins, drowsy, emptied, blot, cinched) into subsequent rooms!

Cognitive Architecture · LEGOS + Bratman (1987) / Rao & Georgeff (1995) + Park et al. (2023)

THE MINDS: Giving a Faceless Flour Sack Beliefs, Desires, Plans, and Reflections

How do you give a 2D flour sack an inspectable mind without handing authorship over to an unrepeatable cloud LLM? agents.js fuses three classic AI lineages into 67 lines of deterministic JavaScript:

  1. B — Beliefs (Epistemic PLoT): What this specific sack currently estimates about the other three tenants' str (strength, 0..1) and harm (danger, 0..1), based solely on deeds it participated in, witnessed in lit rooms, snooped via keyhole, or uncovered from room traces.
  2. D — Desires (LEGOS Goal + Temperament-Weighted Affect): Each sack holds a permanent LEGOS life goal plus pairwise affects (fear, guilt, grudge, desire, debt, pride) scaled by its personal TEMPER—for example, Widow Roessler nurses grudges (0.90) with almost no guilt (0.25), whereas Belindo feels crushing guilt (1.00) and mild grudge (0.40).
  3. I — Intentions (Bratman 1987; Rao & Georgeff 1995): Desires pull in many directions at once; an Intention is a committed plan (make X pay, win X, or make it right with X) that multiplies matching deed utilities by 2.2× and persists across rooms until discharged (Done: X.).
  4. Memory & Reflection Stream (Joon Sung Park et al., “Generative Agents”, 2023): Every known event appends a timestamped record shown as [Deed#] [Kind: O/R/P] [Importance 1..10]:
    • O (Observation): e.g., 2 O 8 Roessler drugged me.
    • R (Reflection): Triggered automatically whenever accumulated importance crosses 14! The sack tallies who has weighed heaviest on its mind and writes higher-level syntheses (“Roessler is dangerous.”, “The house sees me as the scapegoat.”).
    • P (Plan): Commits a new BDI intention into the stream. When two sacks meet again, retrieve() ranks memories by Recency(0.85Δt) + Importance/10 + Relevance, highlighting active memories in ember red and flashing them onto the film canvas!

Egoism & Tragic Arc · Akira Kurosawa, Rashomon (1950) & Something Like an Autobiography (1982)

THE LIES · Kurosawa: Protective Self-Deceptions and What Shatters Them

Why is the penultimate section of the Film Bank called THE LIES · Kurosawa? In his memoir Something Like an Autobiography (1982), explaining what drove Rashomon (1950), Akira Kurosawa described the human heart's inability to live—or recount its own story—without an egoistic protective fiction:

“Human beings are unable to be honest with themselves about themselves. They cannot talk about themselves without embellishing. This script portrays such human beings—the kind who cannot survive without lies to make them feel they are better people than they really are.” — Akira Kurosawa (1982)

In Rashomon, each witness bends the grove's violence around their vanity (strength, honor, modesty). In story.js (CAST & BREAKS) and array.html's CHARACTER view, each of Calvino's four tenants enters the boarding house clinging to a protective life-lie—an equation about what keeps them safe or blameless:

  • Widow Roessler: “Possession equals safety.” Broken the first time she is still alive and targeted as victim of blackmail, prostitution, knifing, assault, or slander—proving her deeds and strongbox shield nothing.
  • Belindo Kid: “Strength makes me safe.” Broken when the wrestler is felled not by fair muscle, but by chemistry, rope, despair, or a revolver (victim of drugging, tying, incitement, or gun).
  • Inigo: “Nothing I do counts.” Unlike the others, Inigo's lie is aristocratic detachment—so it is broken only when he himself acts as perpetrator of an irreversible lethal deed (knifing, strangling, or incitement).
  • Ogiva: “Being looked at is being safe.” Broken when visibility turns into invasive humiliation or violence (victim of slander, snooping, assault, or knifing).

Memory, Chance, Consequence & Transition Functions: Assembling Continuous Cinema

If twelve autonomous SCION swarms build twelve deeds in wildly different visual languages—from Lotte Reiniger shadow cut-outs to spring pendulums, Yuri Norstein fog glass, and ARAP triangle meshes—why doesn't a film look like twelve disconnected clips? Because film.html composes 41 procedural modules across a strict state joint:

Beat k = [Chapter Card 1.8s] → [Memory Beat 2.5s] → [Transition 2–3s] → [Deed 6.0s + pre/post + .1s/.4s Memory Flashes] → [Consequence Beat 2.5s]
  • 4 Character Memory Engines (memory/{R,B,I,O}.js): Each tenant remembers in the medium of its own psychology! Roessler (SCION-38) runs a Viterbi dynamic-programming decoder over 10 fixed ledger-keeper drawings; Belindo (SCION-37) binds crops of past deeds as textures onto his 6-bone skinned cloth body; Inigo (SCION-10) warps stills via Moving Least Squares into dreamy distortion; Ogiva (SCION-26) views past deeds inside polygon-masked mirror shards.
  • 13 Continuity Transitions (trans/00..12.js): Every transition renders the exact final frame (t = 5.99s) of Deed k−1 and the exact opening frame (t = 0.00s) of Deed k (with levers & body marks applied!), classifies sacks as STAY, LEAVE, or ENTER, inserts a mid-walk hesitation stall if an entering sack carries a heavy memory of the tenant it is about to face, and rewrites the room via Collage Graph Rewriting, Live2D Cubism, MLS mesh warps, Bounded Biharmonic Weights, Moho Smart Bones, or After Effects corner-pin mattes!
  • 12 Consequence Modules (conseq/01..12.js): Stage the forensic aftermath—Provenance DAGs (01), Alpha-Matte drug waking (02), GrabCut evidence extraction (04), Toon Boom needle-and-thread mending behind a bolted door (08), and SAM-2 mask proposals (11).
  • Two Levels of Chance & Granular Sound: At the macro level, PIE entropy τ governs choice stochasticity while OPENINGS halts where Shannon ambiguity peaks; at the micro kinematic level (VTX-0.1), exogenous physical chance (an invariant 18 N floor jolt in chance_trace.json) is held fixed across counterfactual takes so viewer attribution isolates intent. And in kit.js, every sound is synthesized live from grains of cloth and flour (R 600Hz creak, B 45Hz thump, I 2400Hz breath, O 5200Hz glass triad).
